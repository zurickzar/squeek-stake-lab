Learning lessons

Lesson 1 — Blockchain basics

A blockchain is a shared digital record maintained by a network of computers. Transactions are grouped into blocks and linked together.

Remember: A transaction being visible on-chain does not automatically mean a project or contract is safe.

Lesson 2 — Wallet safety

A wallet lets you manage blockchain accounts and authorize transactions.

Safety rule: Never share your seed phrase or private key. Check the network, destination, and requested permissions before signing.

Lesson 3 — Staking

Staking generally means committing eligible tokens to support a blockchain mechanism or staking protocol.

Rewards, lock periods, withdrawal rules, and risks depend on the specific network and protocol.

Lesson 4 — Smart contracts

Smart contracts are programs deployed on a blockchain. Interacting with one can change on-chain state or move assets.

Read the contract details and understand the transaction before approving it.
